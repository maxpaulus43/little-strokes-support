Privacy policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Little Strokes is a Hanzi handwriting practice app for iPad. Searching, practicing, and local profiles work without an account or an internet connection. There are no ads, analytics, or tracking. Optionally, a parent can turn on sync with a Google account so practice follows the family between iPads.

Practice on your iPad

Your profile names, selected characters, review progress, pen and grid settings, and handwriting are saved on your iPad. Profiles separate learners but are not separate online accounts. Deleting the app removes its local data. Apple's device backup services may include this data according to your device settings.

The character dictionary and stroke illustrations are bundled with the app, so searching and practicing never require a network connection. Apple Pencil and finger input are used for drawing on the worksheet.

Optional Google sign-in

Signing in is optional and only affects sync. A parent chooses Account & sync in the app and signs in with Google. Google handles the sign-in under Google's privacy policy; the developer never receives your Google password. The app asks only for your Google account identifier and email address, which it stores with your sign-in tokens in the iPad's Keychain, protected by the device passcode.

The app does not ask for names, ages, birthdates, or contact details. Use a parent's Google account, not a child's. One account shares one practice library, including every learner's profile, with every iPad signed in to it.

Cloud sync

Signing in alone uploads nothing. When a parent turns on Sync automatically, the app sends these to a Cloudflare service operated for Little Strokes: profile names, selected characters, review progress, pen and grid settings, and handwriting. This is used only to store and transfer practice between your devices. Google verifies your identity, and the service keeps one practice library per Google account. The service does not store your email address or your sign-in tokens.

Sync runs after writing pauses and when the app opens, returns to the foreground, or goes to the background. If two iPads change the same learner, the app asks which version to keep and replaces the other. Before sync changes an iPad, that iPad's previous practice is saved locally as a recovery copy.

Sync data is protected in transit with HTTPS and at rest by the provider, but it is not end-to-end encrypted. If two iPads change the same learner, the app asks which version to keep. Google and Cloudflare process network information, such as IP addresses, to provide their services. Little Strokes' sync service does not write practice content or sign-in tokens to logs, but Google and Cloudflare may keep security and operational records, such as request times and IP addresses, under their own policies.

Deleting cloud data

In Account & sync, choose Delete cloud practice to remove the cloud library, turn off sync on every iPad, and sign out on that iPad. A small marker remains so an older iPad cannot silently restore deleted practice. Provider backups may retain deleted data for a limited time under their retention policies. Each iPad keeps the practice already saved on it, and local recovery copies or older local files may still contain earlier practice. Delete cloud practice before removing the app if you also want to remove the cloud copy.

You can also sign out at any time, which stops sync on that iPad and keeps its practice, or revoke the app's access to your Google account in your Google account settings.

Actions you choose

Exporting a PDF, exporting the bundled character data, or opening an external link shares only what you choose with the destination you select, under that destination's privacy practices.

If you contact support, the information you choose to provide is used to respond to your request. Do not include a child's personal details or private handwriting unless needed to explain an issue. Apple operates the App Store and may process diagnostics and device information under Apple's privacy policy and your device preferences.

Children and adults

Little Strokes can be used by children and adults. Without an account, the app does not request any personal information. Sync is meant to be set up by a parent, and it stores practice under the parent's own Google account rather than asking a child to sign in.

This support website
