Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Little Strokes is an offline Hanzi handwriting practice app for iPad. The app does not collect or transmit your personal data, writing, character searches, or settings to its developer. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or third-party network SDKs.

Your practice stays on your iPad

Your selected characters, pen and grid settings, and handwriting are saved in the app's local storage on your iPad. Deleting the app removes that local data. Apple's device backup services may include this data according to your device settings; Little Strokes does not operate a separate cloud or synchronization service.

The character dictionary and stroke illustrations are bundled with the app. Searching and practicing do not require an internet connection. Apple Pencil input is used for drawing, and fingers are used for worksheet navigation and shortcuts.

Actions you choose

If you choose to export the bundled character data or open an external credits link, the destination you select handles that action under its own privacy practices. Little Strokes does not transmit your handwriting as part of exporting the character library.

If you contact support, the information you choose to provide is used to respond to your request. Do not include a child's personal details or private handwriting unless needed to explain an issue. Apple operates the App Store and may process purchases, diagnostics, and device information under Apple's privacy policy and your device preferences.

Children and adults

Little Strokes can be used by children and adults. The app does not request names, ages, contact information, or other personal information from users.

This support website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub handles technical request information under its privacy statement. This website has no advertising, analytics scripts, forms, or tracking cookies added by Little Strokes.

Contact

For questions about privacy, email support@chefness.org.
