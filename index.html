One stroke at a time.

A calm place to practice Chinese characters with your iPad and Apple Pencil.

Search 9,574 Hanzi by character, pinyin, or English meaning. See each stroke separately, then practice on a grid you can make your own.

What do I need?

An iPad running iPadOS 17 or later and an Apple Pencil compatible with your iPad. Apple Pencil is required for writing. The character library works offline.

How do I add characters?

Tap Add characters, search, select the characters you want, and tap Add to grid. Try 月, yue, or moon. Pasting a Chinese word finds its supported characters.

How do I move around the page?

Use one finger to pan and two fingers to pinch and zoom. Tap Fit page to see the full worksheet. Fingers cannot draw or select handwriting.

How do I undo or redo?

Tap with two fingers to undo or three fingers to redo. Repeat taps to step through strokes. The toolbar buttons do the same thing. History applies to the most recently used square.

Can I change the pen and grid?

Tap the sliders button at the top right. Choose a pen, pencil, or marker, then change its color, thickness, and opacity. Choose 1–12 practice squares per character and how many contain gray tracing guides.

Can I rearrange or hide characters?

Long press a chip in Your Grid and drag it onto another chip to rearrange your characters. Tap a chip and switch Show on grid off to hide its row. Its position and writing stay saved.

Where does my practice go?

Your writing, selected characters, and settings save automatically on your iPad. Deleting the app removes its local data. Device backups depend on your Apple settings.

Need help?

Email support@chefness.org for Little Strokes support.

When reporting an issue, include your iPad model, iPadOS version, app version, and what happened. Avoid including a child's personal information or private handwriting.
